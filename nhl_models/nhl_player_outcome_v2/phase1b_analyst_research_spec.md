# Phase1B independent intelligence gate

A professional forecast asks who will dress, which team owns that identity, how EV/PP/PK deployment will change, how many attempts those deployments create, and which attempts reach the net. Each observation must carry source, authority, publication/retrieval time, freshness, uncertainty and missingness. Roster membership does not certify dressing. Prior role does not certify today's line. Uncaptured injury/coach/goalie information remains missing.

Skill history follows a stable NHL ID; current-role history stays within the current team. Rest follows the schedule. Travel distance, coaching changes, competitive balance, zone starts, PP shooting involvement and confirmed goalie state are blocked unless an authorized timed source supplies them; do not synthesize them from outcomes. Existing total SOG and TOI support role/skill baselines, not attempts or zone opportunity.

The scripted transparent analyst benchmark uses only legal prior statistics and is available early in a season. It is not an independent professional analyst. The independent comparator intake requires a prospectively signed forecast, full PMF, author/method version and evidence hashes at the same cutoff. No October7 human forecasts will be reconstructed.

Preserve the B2 28-day/15000 meaningful T90 requirement. Phase1B has separate component and forward gates; its research code is never production serving. Failed families stay frozen. A partial TOI implementation is not a certified complete hockey engine.
